About

Code that's built to be trusted with your name on it.

I'm a full-stack developer who cares about the details most people skip past — the loading state that never feels slow, the layout that holds up on a five-year-old phone, the form that tells you exactly what went wrong instead of just failing quietly.

I work across the whole stack: interfaces people enjoy clicking through, and the backend logic, databases, and APIs that keep everything running underneath. My goal on every project is the same — ship something that looks considered and works properly, on time.
